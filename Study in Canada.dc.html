Study in Canada from India: costs, colleges, visas and work rights

Reviewed by Kanan Canada desk·Updated 3 Oct 2026·9 min read
boltQuick answer

Most Indian students spend ₹22–40 lakh a year on tuition and living in Canada. You need an offer from a designated learning institution (DLI), a provincial attestation letter (PAL) and proof of funds. After graduating, you can work on a post-graduation work permit (PGWP) for up to 3 years.
